# Privacy Engineering

## Child photo
Temporary processing input, not permanent profile asset. Delete according to documented retention.

## Adult voice
Explicit consent, adult-only policy, provider verification where applicable, deletion workflow, retention policy and provider-terms review.

## Audio
Private storage and controlled application URLs.

## QR
Stable platform URL/token → authorization → current audio asset. Never expose a permanent raw storage URL as the QR target.

## Deletion
Support deletion of account, family, child metadata, books, generated assets, voice profile, audio and QR access, subject to legal/accounting retention requirements.
