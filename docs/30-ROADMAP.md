# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Architecture
Domain model, database, provider interfaces, RLS/security, documentation.

## Phase 1 — Real MVP
Persistent accounts/books, asynchronous generation, product catalog, physical variant, real checkout, verified webhook, one fulfillment provider, print-ready files, tracking, customer order page, digital copy.

## Phase 2 — Reliability
Second fulfillment provider, provider metrics, retries/reconciliation, refunds/replacements.

## Phase 3 — Voice
Adult consent, provider adapter, voice lifecycle, audio generation, private storage, QR playback.

## Phase 4 — Globalization
Countries, currencies, languages, localized products, tax configuration, printer routing, carriers.

## Phase 5 — Mobile
iOS/Android on shared backend/API.

## Phase 6 — Subscription
Credits, renewal, cancellation, entitlement system, additional copies.
