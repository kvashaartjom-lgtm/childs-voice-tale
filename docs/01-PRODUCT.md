# Product Specification

## Primary product
Personalized physical book.

## Included
Digital copy of the purchased book.

## Optional/future
- Adult family voice narration
- QR-linked audio
- Subscription/book credits
- Additional copies
- Premium physical formats

## User journey
Discover → configure → generate → preview → select product → checkout → payment → fulfillment → shipment → delivery.

## Child data
Child profiles may contain name/age metadata. Original child photos are temporary processing inputs, not permanent profile assets.

## Voice
Adult voices only. Child voice cloning is not a product feature.

## Product catalog
Physical formats are configurable product variants, e.g. A5/A4, hardcover, 32/48 pages. Dimensions, production cost, price, eligible countries and fulfillment providers are data, not hard-coded UI constants.
