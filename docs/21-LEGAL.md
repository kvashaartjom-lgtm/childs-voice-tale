# Legal Workstream

Engineering checklist, not legal advice.

Initial launch: Netherlands/EU.

Workstreams:
1. Business entity/tax
2. VAT
3. Consumer terms
4. Privacy/cookies
5. Refund/withdrawal rules for personalized goods
6. Digital-content terms
7. AI disclosure/terms
8. Adult voice consent/data processing
9. DPAs
10. International transfers
11. IP/copyright/licensing
12. Apple/Google compliance
13. Payment provider compliance
14. Print/fulfillment contracts

System must support versioned terms, consent and configurable tax/country rules.
