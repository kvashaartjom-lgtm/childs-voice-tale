# Legal & Privacy Requirements

This is an engineering/product checklist, not legal advice. Final launch policies require qualified legal review.

## Potential data
Account, billing/shipping, child metadata, temporary child photos, adult voice recordings, generated books/audio, order/payment metadata.

## Child photos
Original photos are used for generation and are not permanent profile assets. Delete after the processing purpose is complete, subject to documented operational/security retention.

## Adult voice
Require explicit consent, adult-only policy, clear explanation, provider verification where required, deletion mechanism, retention policy and provider-terms review.

## Security
Provider secrets/API keys are server-side only.

## Required capabilities
Access/export where applicable, correction, deletion, account closure and deletion of voice/media data.

## EU launch
Design for GDPR principles. Final legal basis, controller/processor roles, DPAs, international transfers, retention and child-data requirements require legal review.
