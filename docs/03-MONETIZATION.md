# Monetization

## MVP
One personalized physical book + digital copy.

Do not make subscriptions a dependency of the first successful purchase/fulfillment loop.

## Future
- annual subscription
- book credits
- additional copies
- premium formats
- premium audio
- gifts/family plans

## Pricing
Backend calculates authoritative:
product price + shipping + tax - discount = total.

Never use a single global `BOOK_PRICE` as the business source of truth.
